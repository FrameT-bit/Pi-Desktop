where it lives

Sidebar: summary, an active section with state, sparkline, and time, plus recent with unread items. In chat: a “now” card with the current tool and the approval strip.

what changes

State is explicit: run, wait, done. The session that needs you is the only amber thing on screen; the rest stays quiet. The card collapses into the receipt when the turn closes.

multi-session

This concept is built for parallelism: three active sessions, one waiting for permission. The approval strip brings the background session forward without switching tabs; the waiting tab gets “!”.
